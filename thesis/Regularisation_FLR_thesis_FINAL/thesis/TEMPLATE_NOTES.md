# Layout and template attribution

This revision follows the numerical layout specifications in the supplied University of Bonn Economics thesis instructions dated 23 July 2026 and the recommended [Holger Gerhardt thesis template](https://github.com/HolgerGerhardt/ThesisTemplates), inspected on 13 September 2026.

- A4, one-sided; left/right margins 2.5/4 cm; top/bottom margins 2.8/2.8 cm.
- Main text: 11 pt Times-compatible font, 19.5 pt baseline; text height 682.5 pt (35 baselines).
- Tables, captions, and bibliography: 10 pt with 15 pt baseline.
- Footnotes: 9 pt with 13.5 pt baseline.
- First three heading levels: 14/12/11 pt, bold, with the template's nominal spacing.
- Paragraph indentation: 19.5 pt; no extra paragraph spacing; font expansion disabled.
- Consecutive Arabic page numbers from the introduction through the declaration. Roman numbering is used for preliminary pages.

This is an adaptation, not the unmodified template. The original report-class chapter counters are retained, with continuous main chapters to match the template's article-style flow. `mathptmx` provides Times-compatible text and mathematics consistently across bibliography backends; the reference template instead uses NewTX scaled to 0.995. The revision uses the full nominal font size. `raggedbottom` leaves unused space at page bottoms rather than stretching gaps within pages; it does not increase the text area or reduce line spacing. Appendix text retains the main-text font and baseline. Current university instructions take precedence over the template's older appendix numbering conventions.
